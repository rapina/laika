# Murr · Garden of Gaps

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: Sputnik Workshop Earth player-review
artwork, landscape 3:2. Preserve Murr exactly as the same tabby cat from
`brand/art/murr-base.png`: facial markings, proportions, realistic engraved fur,
quiet observant personality. Place Murr at the old radio-room desk at night, one
front paw cautiously parting a dense miniature field of dry ochre reeds. Several

attentive rather than comic. A tiny dark beetle is partly visible below the reeds
but has not reached the wet leaf. Keep the reference's vintage etched
printmaking texture, near-black and warm ochre palette, with teal as the only
accent. Wide composition, Murr left-center and reeds across the desk, restrained
lighting, no UI, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no watermark, no extra
animals.
